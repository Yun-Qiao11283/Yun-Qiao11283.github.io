## Topological Data Analysis (TDA) for Equity Markets

**September 2025 – Present** · The Ohio State University<br>
Supervised by **Ph.D. Candidate Krnic Luci**

### Research question

Traditional financial models can struggle to capture high-dimensional structural shifts during macroeconomic shocks. This project investigates whether **persistent homology** can characterize those changes in a market manifold comprising core U.S. equities and exogenous safe-haven assets.

### Approach

I am developing a **Python-based computational pipeline** to:

1. Represent the market structure through simplicial complexes.
2. Apply persistent homology to study its topological features.
3. Extract the **L₁ norm of persistence landscapes** to quantify topological phase transitions.

### Objective & current status

The long-term objective is to develop a robust, threshold-free early warning system for systemic market crashes. **The computational pipeline is currently under development.**
